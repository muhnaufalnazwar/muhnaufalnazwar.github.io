About me

Accounting student focused on auditing and audit quality.

I am studying for a Bachelor of Applied Accounting (D4 Akuntansi) at Universitas Negeri Makassar, currently holding a GPA of 3.84/4.00. In 2024 I worked as a Junior Assistant Auditor at KAP ASRI, supporting senior auditors with data collection, preliminary analysis, stock opname and reporting of findings, and in 2026 I joined PT Kawasan Industri Makassar (PT KIMA) as a Finance Intern in its Container Depot unit.

I have co-authored three journal articles covering auditing, taxation, and human resource management.

Outside the classroom I lead the Research and Development division of MP Ekolibrium FEB UNM and have chaired and coordinated several student committees. Based in Makassar, Indonesia.
